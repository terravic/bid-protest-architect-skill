# Comprehensive Bid Protest Grounds Taxonomy

This reference categorizes the 8 primary bid protest grounds recognized by the GAO and the U.S. Court of Federal Claims, detailing the required evidence, typical agency defenses, and winning argument patterns.

---

## Taxonomy Overview Table

| Ground ID | Ground Name | Core Statutory / FAR Basis | Required Documentary Evidence | Key Burden of Proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **GR-01** | **Unstated Evaluation Criteria** | FAR 15.303(a)(2), FAR 15.305(a) | Section M vs. TET Report / SSDD showing penalty/strength for unlisted item | The assigned weakness/strength is not intrinsically related to or encompassed by stated RFP factors. |
| **GR-02** | **Disparate / Unequal Treatment** | FAR 1.102-2(c)(3), FAR 15.305 | Side-by-side proposal excerpts and TET scoring comments showing identical features rated differently | Offerors proposed substantially similar solutions, but agency evaluated them disparately without rational justification. |
| **GR-03** | **Relaxation of Mandatory Requirement** | FAR 15.206, FAR 15.305 | Section L/M mandatory clause ("shall/must") vs. Awardee non-compliant proposal | Awardee failed to satisfy a mandatory minimum specification, yet agency accepted it without RFP amendment. |
| **GR-04** | **Flawed Past Performance Evaluation** | FAR 15.305(a)(2) | Section M relevance definitions vs. Awardee past performance references | References accepted by agency lacked required size, scope, or complexity, or agency ignored negative CPARS. |
| **GR-05** | **Defective Best Value Tradeoff** | FAR 15.308 | SSDD text, evaluation matrices, price delta | SSA conducted mechanical score comparison or failed to articulate reasonable basis for tradeoff where technical outweighed price. |
| **GR-06** | **Misleading / Unequal Discussions** | FAR 15.306(d), FAR 15.307 | ENs (Evaluation Notices), Discussion transcripts, FPR | Agency misled protester into changing a compliant approach or failed to raise a significant weakness while helping awardee. |
| **GR-07** | **Organizational Conflict of Interest (OCI)** | FAR 9.5, FAR 9.505 | Teaming agreements, prior statement of work, agency relationship records | Awardee had unequal access to non-public information, biased ground rules, or impaired objectivity not mitigated under FAR 9.5. |
| **GR-08** | **Flawed Cost / Price Realism** | FAR 15.404-1(d) | Cost worksheets, RFP Section M realism mandate, Labor rate benchmarks | Agency failed to analyze whether awardee's low price reflected lack of technical understanding or performance risk. |
