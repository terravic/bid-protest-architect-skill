# Bid Protest Viability & Standing Checklist

Use this checklist prior to generating filing documents to confirm that the protest meets statutory, jurisdictional, and procedural requirements before the GAO or U.S. Court of Federal Claims.

---

## 1. Timeliness Verification (4 C.F.R. 21.2)

- [ ] **Solicitation Defect**: Was the issue a patent ambiguity or defect on the face of the RFP?
  - *Requirement*: Must be filed prior to the deadline for receipt of initial proposals.
- [ ] **Required Debriefing Requested**: Was a post-award debriefing requested in writing within three (3) calendar days of notice of award (FAR 15.506(a)(1))?
- [ ] **Debriefing Q&A**: Were written debriefing questions submitted within the agency's specified window?
- [ ] **CICA Stay Window (5 Days)**: Is the protest being filed within 5 calendar days of debriefing conclusion to trigger the automatic stay of contract performance (31 U.S.C. 3553(d)(4))?
- [ ] **GAO Timeliness Window (10 Days)**: Is the protest being filed within 10 calendar days of debriefing conclusion or when the basis of protest was known (4 C.F.R. 21.2(a)(2))?

---

## 2. Interested Party Standing (4 C.F.R. 21.0(a))

- [ ] **Actual Offeror**: Did the protester submit a timely proposal?
- [ ] **Direct Economic Interest**: Would the protester stand in line for contract award if the protest is sustained?
- [ ] **Intervening Offeror Check**: If intermediate offerors exist between protester and awardee, are their evaluations or eligibility also subject to challenge?

---

## 3. Substantive Evidentiary Basis & Competitive Prejudice

- [ ] **Prejudice Standard**: But for the agency error, would the protester have had a substantial chance of receiving the award (404 F.3d 1346)?
- [ ] **Contemporaneous Record Evidence**: Does documentary evidence in the RFP, TET report, SSDD, or debriefing substantiate each factual assertion?
- [ ] **Objective Legal Defect**: Does each count establish arbitrary action or violation of procurement law rather than subjective disagreement with scoring?
