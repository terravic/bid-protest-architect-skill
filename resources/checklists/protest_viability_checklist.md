# Bid Protest Viability & Standing Checklist

Use this checklist prior to generating filing documents to confirm that the protest meets all statutory, jurisdictional, and procedural requirements before the GAO or Court of Federal Claims.

---

## 1. Timeliness Verification (4 C.F.R. § 21.2)

- [ ] **Solicitation Defect**: Was the issue a patent ambiguity or defect in the RFP?
  - *Requirement*: Must have been filed prior to proposal submission due date.
- [ ] **Required Debriefing Requested**: Was a post-award debriefing requested within three (3) days of award notice (FAR 15.506(a)(1))?
- [ ] **Enhanced Debriefing Q&A**: Were debriefing questions submitted timely pursuant to agency instructions?
- [ ] **CICA Stay Window (5 Days)**: Is the protest being filed within 5 calendar days of debriefing completion to trigger automatic stay of contract performance (31 U.S.C. § 3553(d)(4))?
- [ ] **GAO Timeliness Window (10 Days)**: Is the protest being filed within 10 calendar days of debriefing completion or when basis of protest was known (4 C.F.R. § 21.2(a)(2))?

---

## 2. Interested Party Standing (4 C.F.R. § 21.0(a))

- [ ] **Actual Offeror**: Did the protester submit a timely, conforming proposal?
- [ ] **Direct Economic Interest**: Would the protester be in line for contract award if the protest is sustained?
- [ ] **Intervening Offeror Check**: If there are other intermediate offerors between protester and awardee, are their evaluations also flawed or challengeable?

---

## 3. Substantive Evidentiary Basis & Competitive Prejudice

- [ ] **Prejudice Standard**: But for the alleged agency error, would the protester have had a substantial chance of receiving award?
- [ ] **Contemporaneous Record Evidence**: Is there documentary evidence in the RFP, TET report, SSDD, or Debriefing supporting each factual assertion?
- [ ] **Not Mere Disagreement**: Does the claim demonstrate arbitrary/capricious action or violation of law rather than subjective disagreement with technical scores?
