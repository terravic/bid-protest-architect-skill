## 3. Validated Grounds for Protest

### COUNT I: Unstated Evaluation Criteria in Factor 1 (Technical & Cloud Architecture Approach)
- **Ground Classification**: `GR-01` | **Viability Rating**: **96/100**
- **Statutory / FAR Authority**: FAR 15.303(a)(2), FAR 15.305(a)
- **Key GAO Precedents**: *GAO Decision B-400760; GAO Decision B-416916*
- **Factual Record & Evidence**:
  > RFP Section M and Section L set forth the Technical & Cloud Architecture Approach evaluation requirements without mentioning or requiring 'dedicated on-premises physical datacenter hardware bridge facility to connect with legacy Baltimore CMS mainframe hardware.' The TET assigned a 'Significant Weakness' to Protester (Apex) on the ground that Apex did not propose 'dedicated on-premises physical datacenter hardware bridge facility to connect with legacy Baltimore CMS mainframe hardware.' Neither Section L nor Section M mentions or requires this feature.
- **Agency Error**:
  > The Agency penalized Protester based on an undisclosed criterion that was not reasonably encompassed by the stated evaluation factors in Section M.
- **Competitive Prejudice Analysis (FAR 15.308 / Substantial Chance Test)**:
  > The contemporaneous evaluation record confirms this Significant Weakness caused Apex's Factor 1 rating degradation from Outstanding to Acceptable. Removing this unlawful weakness elevates Apex to Outstanding under the most heavily weighted evaluation factor.
- **Adversarial Critique & Rebuttal to Agency Defense**:
  > Anticipated Agency Defense: The agency will argue that the unstated feature is 'logically related' to the general technical scope. Rebuttal: GAO precedent strictly prohibits agencies from introducing material infrastructure or architectural mandates under general technical criteria without notice in Section M (GAO B-400760).

### COUNT II: Improper Waiver of Mandatory Key Personnel Qualifications for Awardee (Factor 2)
- **Ground Classification**: `GR-03` | **Viability Rating**: **98/100**
- **Statutory / FAR Authority**: FAR 15.206, FAR 15.305
- **Key GAO Precedents**: *GAO Decision B-400394; GAO Decision B-404153*
- **Factual Record & Evidence**:
  > RFP Section L.4 explicitly mandated that Lead Cloud Solutions Architect possess a minimum of 10 years of direct federal cloud experience and required professional/security certifications, warning that failure to satisfy mandatory minimums would result in an 'Unacceptable' rating. Nexus proposed Johnathan Brooks, who possessed only 4 years of experience and lacked mandatory certifications, yet the TET waived the deficiency and awarded Nexus an 'Acceptable' rating.
- **Agency Error**:
  > The Agency waived a mandatory minimum solicitation requirement for the Awardee without amending the RFP or affording Protester the same opportunity to propose relaxed staffing.
- **Competitive Prejudice Analysis (FAR 15.308 / Substantial Chance Test)**:
  > Under FAR 15.206 and established GAO rule, a proposal that fails to meet a mandatory minimum qualification is non-compliant and ineligible for award. Nexus should have been eliminated from the competition.
- **Adversarial Critique & Rebuttal to Agency Defense**:
  > Anticipated Agency Defense: The agency has discretion in evaluating candidate qualifications. Rebuttal: Where an RFP specifies mandatory minimum years of experience and certifications ('shall possess minimum 10 years'), the agency has no legal discretion to waive the requirement post-submission (GAO B-404153).

### COUNT III: Acceptance of Technically Non-Compliant Availability SLA from Awardee (Factor 1)
- **Ground Classification**: `GR-03` | **Viability Rating**: **95/100**
- **Statutory / FAR Authority**: FAR 15.206, FAR 15.305
- **Key GAO Precedents**: *GAO Decision B-416211; GAO Decision B-400394*
- **Factual Record & Evidence**:
  > RFP Section L.3.1 established a mandatory minimum availability SLA threshold of 99.99% monthly uptime, explicitly stating that proposals offering below 99.99% 'will be deemed non-compliant and ineligible for award.' Nexus expressly proposed a 99.95% monthly uptime SLA. The TET failed to assign a deficiency and awarded Nexus a 'Good' rating.
- **Agency Error**:
  > The Agency awarded the contract to an offeror whose proposal took explicit exception to a material performance threshold.
- **Competitive Prejudice Analysis (FAR 15.308 / Substantial Chance Test)**:
  > Nexus's proposal was facially non-compliant with a mandatory solicitation term and should have been rejected as ineligible.
- **Adversarial Critique & Rebuttal to Agency Defense**:
  > Anticipated Agency Defense: 99.95% was deemed functionally adequate during evaluation. Rebuttal: An agency cannot unilaterally relax a mandatory RFP performance requirement during evaluation without amending the solicitation (GAO B-416211).

### COUNT IV: Unreasonable Past Performance Evaluation of Awardee (Factor 3)
- **Ground Classification**: `GR-04` | **Viability Rating**: **88/100**
- **Statutory / FAR Authority**: FAR 15.305(a)(2)
- **Key GAO Precedents**: *GAO Decision B-414223; GAO Decision B-402567*
- **Factual Record & Evidence**:
  > RFP Section L.5 and Section M defined 'relevant' past performance as federal contracts with value >= $50,000,000 and matching regulatory/mission scope. Nexus submitted non-relevant commercial/state references ($3.20M, $8.50M), yet the TET assigned Nexus a 'Satisfactory Confidence (Very Good)' rating.
- **Agency Error**:
  > The Agency failed to evaluate past performance in accordance with the solicitation's stated criteria for size, scope, and complexity.
- **Competitive Prejudice Analysis (FAR 15.308 / Substantial Chance Test)**:
  > Proper evaluation under Section M relevance thresholds would have yielded Limited or Neutral Confidence for Nexus, widening Apex's substantial non-price superiority (Apex held Substantial Confidence including a $91.20M relevant contract).
- **Adversarial Critique & Rebuttal to Agency Defense**:
  > Anticipated Agency Defense: Evaluators possess broad latitude in assessing past performance relevance. Rebuttal: Assigning a favorable confidence rating to commercial references an order of magnitude below the stated RFP dollar floor violates FAR 15.305(a)(2) (GAO B-414223).

### COUNT V: Defective Best Value Cost-Technical Tradeoff Decision (FAR 15.308)
- **Ground Classification**: `GR-05` | **Viability Rating**: **92/100**
- **Statutory / FAR Authority**: FAR 15.308, FAR 15.101-1
- **Key GAO Precedents**: *GAO Decision B-293134; GAO Decision B-415755*
- **Factual Record & Evidence**:

- **Agency Error**:

- **Competitive Prejudice Analysis (FAR 15.308 / Substantial Chance Test)**:

- **Adversarial Critique & Rebuttal to Agency Defense**:
  > Anticipated Agency Defense: The SSA exercises broad business judgment in cost-technical tradeoffs. Rebuttal: Broad discretion cannot salvage a tradeoff decision predicated on unlawful underlying evaluation errors or devoid of qualitative rationale (GAO B-293134).
