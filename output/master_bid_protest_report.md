## 3. Validated Grounds for Protest

### COUNT I: Unstated Evaluation Criteria in Factor 1 (Technical & Cloud Architecture)
- **Ground Classification**: `GR-01` | **Viability Rating**: **96/100**
- **Statutory / FAR Authority**: FAR 15.303(a)(2), FAR 15.305(a)
- **Key GAO Precedents**: *Alion Science & Technology Corp., B-400760; Vistronix, LLC, B-416916*
- **Factual Record & Evidence**:
  > RFP Section M.1 and L.3 set forth the cloud architecture evaluation requirements, focusing on FedRAMP High multi-region deployment and Zero Trust. The TET assigned a 'Significant Weakness' to Protester (Apex) on the sole ground that Apex did not propose an 'on-premises physical datacenter hardware bridge facility for legacy Baltimore CMS mainframe hardware.' Neither Section L nor Section M mentions or requires any on-premises physical datacenter hardware bridge.
- **Agency Error**:
  > The Agency penalized Protester based on an undisclosed criterion that was not reasonably encompassed by the stated evaluation factors in Section M.
- **Competitive Prejudice Analysis (FAR 15.308 / Bannum Test)**:
  > The TET explicitly noted that this single Significant Weakness was the sole reason Apex's Factor 1 rating was reduced from Outstanding to Acceptable. Removing this unlawful weakness elevates Apex to Outstanding under the most heavily weighted evaluation factor.
- **Adversarial Critique & Rebuttal to Agency Defense**:
  > Anticipated Agency Defense: The agency will argue that on-premise mainframe connectivity is 'logically related' to healthcare systems. Rebuttal: GAO precedent strictly prohibits agencies from introducing major infrastructure mandates under general technical criteria without prior notice in Section M (Alion Sci. & Tech.).

### COUNT II: Improper Waiver of Mandatory Key Personnel Qualifications for Awardee (Factor 2)
- **Ground Classification**: `GR-03` | **Viability Rating**: **98/100**
- **Statutory / FAR Authority**: FAR 15.206, FAR 15.305
- **Key GAO Precedents**: *ArmorWorks Enterprises, LLC, B-400394; Northeast Military Sales, B-404153*
- **Factual Record & Evidence**:
  > RFP Section L.4 explicitly mandated that Key Personnel Position 1 (Lead Cloud Solutions Architect) 'shall possess a minimum of ten (10) years of direct experience architecting enterprise federal cloud solutions' and maintain active AWS Professional / Google Cloud Fellow and CISSP certifications. Section L.4 warned that failure to meet mandatory minimums would result in an 'Unacceptable' rating. Nexus proposed Johnathan Brooks, who possessed only 4 years of total cloud experience and lacked the mandatory certifications. The TET explicitly acknowledged this deficiency but waived it by stating 'commercial aptitude compensates for the shortage,' awarding Nexus an 'Acceptable' rating.
- **Agency Error**:
  > The Agency waived a mandatory minimum solicitation requirement for the Awardee without amending the RFP or affording Protester the same opportunity to propose alternative staffing.
- **Competitive Prejudice Analysis (FAR 15.308 / Bannum Test)**:
  > Under FAR 15.206 and established GAO rule, a proposal that fails to meet a mandatory minimum qualification is non-compliant and ineligible for award. Nexus should have been eliminated from the competition.
- **Adversarial Critique & Rebuttal to Agency Defense**:
  > Anticipated Agency Defense: The agency has discretion in evaluating candidate qualifications. Rebuttal: Where an RFP specifies mandatory minimum years of experience and certifications ('shall possess minimum 10 years'), the agency has no legal discretion to waive the requirement post-submission (Northeast Military Sales).

## 4. Overall Competitive Prejudice & Legal Conclusion

3. **Defective Tradeoff**: Non-price factors were significantly more important than price. In a lawful re-evaluation, Apex represents the highest-rated compliant offeror.
